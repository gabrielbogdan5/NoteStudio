Your notes, your way.

NoteStudio is a beautifully crafted, offline-first notes app built as a single HTML file. No accounts, no servers, no limits — just a powerful writing experience that works anywhere, anytime.

🎨

3 Themes

PDF Export

Export any note as a styled PDF

🎤

Voice Input

Dictate notes hands-free

🖼️

Image Support

Insert photos from gallery or camera

Fully Offline

Works without internet, always
