01

Creating & Editing Notes

1

New Note

Tap + New Note in the top bar, or tap New in the bottom bar.

2

Add a Title

Tap the Note title… field at the top and type your title. The title appears in the notes list.

3

Write Your Note

Tap the large editor area below the toolbar and start typing. All changes save automatically.

4

Pin a Note

Tap the 📌 pin icon in the note actions row to keep important notes at the top of the list.

5

Delete a Note

Tap the 🗑 trash icon to move to Trash, or tap Delete in the bottom bar. Recover from the Trash folder.

Tip: The date shown next to the note actions row is the last modified time. Toggle it on/off in Settings → Show date.
02

Folders & Organisation

1

Open the Sidebar

Tap the ◀ button on the left edge of the screen, or swipe right from the left edge.

2

Create a Folder

In the sidebar, tap the + button next to FOLDERS. Enter a name and pick a colour.

3

Move a Note to a Folder

Open a note, tap the 🏷 tag icon, then select the folder. A note can belong to one folder.

4

Filter by Folder

Tap a folder in the sidebar to show only notes in that folder. Tap All to go back.

Tip: Use the Pinned and Trash filters in the sidebar for quick access to pinned or deleted notes.
03

Formatting Toolbar

1

Show / Hide Toolbar

Tap HIDE TOOLBAR / SHOW TOOLBAR above the toolbar to toggle it.

2

Text Style

Use the Paragraph dropdown for Heading 1, 2, 3 or normal paragraph. Change size with the number dropdown.

3

Bold / Italic / Underline / Strikethrough

Select text first, then tap B, I, U, or S in the toolbar.

4

Text Colour & Highlight

Col: sets the text colour. Ev: sets the highlight (background) colour. Tap ✕ to clear.

5

Insert Photo

Tap + Foto to insert an image from your gallery or camera. Pinch to resize after inserting.

6

Font Family

Use the Font dropdown to change the typeface for selected text.

04

Export (PDF & Copy)

1

Open Export

Tap Export in the bottom bar while a note is open.

2

Copy All Text

Tap Copy All to copy the plain text of the note to clipboard — ready to paste anywhere.

3

Save as PDF

Tap PDF to open the PDF preview. Tap Save as PDF and choose a location to save.

Tip: The PDF preserves your text colours, headings, and the NoteStudio branding header. Great for sharing formatted notes.
05

Voice Input

1

Enable Microphone

In Settings, make sure Microphone is toggled ON. Grant microphone permission when prompted.

2

Dictate

Open a note, then press and hold the purple 🎤 button. Speak — text appears in real time. Release to stop.

3

Position the Button

In Settings → Microphone position, choose Up, Middle, or Down to move the button where it's comfortable.

Tip: Voice input uses your device language by default. Make sure NoteStudio language matches what you want to dictate in.
06

Settings

1

Open Settings

Tap the ⚙️ gear icon in the top right corner.

2

Language

Choose from English, Română, Polski, or Русский. The entire UI updates instantly.

3

Theme

Pick Dark, Light, or Sepia. The theme applies immediately.

4

Show Date

Toggle the modified date display on/off in the note editor header.

5

Microphone & Left Button Position

Use the Up / Middle / Down selector to position the floating mic button and the sidebar toggle.
